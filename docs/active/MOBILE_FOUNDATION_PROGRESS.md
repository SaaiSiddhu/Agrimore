## Paid-after-expiry release gate

The delivery quote has a ten-minute TTL; Product Credit holds have a thirty-minute TTL. Current `createOrder` refuses either expired object, even with a captured owner-bound payment. New tests establish that such refusal leaves payment consumption, orders, stock, hold, balances and credit ledger unchanged. The saved mobile checkout retains its frozen intent and prevents a new checkout while the first is unresolved. Those controls preserve safety but do not finish fulfillment/refund.

Do not silently drop a hold, change the quoted payable, reacquire credit against a captured payment, reuse an expired quote, or ask the customer to pay again. Completion needs an explicit server-owned resolution: recover an already-created order idempotently, or reconcile/refund an unfulfillable captured checkout with a durable one-use state and a customer-visible outcome. A later retry must use the original request/payment tuple. Ship distance pricing/mobile credit only once expiry, changed pricing/stock and interrupted provider/refund outcomes have connected evidence.
