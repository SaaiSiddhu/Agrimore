# Foundation 11 — Status, progress and time

[Status, progress and time board](01-status-progress-time.png)

One Storybook-style Flutter iOS/Android board generated with built-in image generation. Primary burnt orange `#C2410C`, light/dark specimens and Lucide-style text/icon cues.

Covers online/offline/pending availability, approval states, illustrative offer countdown and expiry, server-confirmed delivery stages, upload progress, payout states, separate delivery-problem and incident-report workflows, timestamps and estimated ETA.

Countdown values and dates are illustrative, not policy. Use actual expiry and measured upload progress; do not infer exact proportions from raster graphics. Application approval is separate from availability. Photo added is not approval. A statement is not paid until transfer is recorded. Reported/recorded is not seen. Returned to seller describes a recorded outcome; reattempt and returned dispositions are alternatives. Missing proof photo does not undo a confirmed delivery. Test actual timing, reduced-motion feedback and transitions in implementation.
