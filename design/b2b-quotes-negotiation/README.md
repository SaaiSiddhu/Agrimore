# Foundation 20 — B2B quotes and negotiation

Eight separate Storybook-style raster mockup boards, generated sequentially with built-in image_gen and visually reviewed. Designed as Flutter iOS/Android concept references with light/dark variants and Lucide-style icons; not implemented React or Flutter components. Outer Storybook shell is documentation only.

Palette: primary green–teal #0F766E; mint #DDF3EA; black #050908; dark surface #0B1513; dark accent #5EEAD4.

## Interaction rules

Quotes are accessed from Home or Account without adding a sixth navigation tab. Actor labels and response status are distinct. An expired offer cannot be accepted; a seller-turn counter may offer new validity. Below-MOQ quantity is advisory in the inspected counter form. Acceptance permits the buyer to place an order and does not imply payment or fulfilment. View order appears only for a linked order.

Source context: seller-mockup-research.md; apps/seller/lib/screens/rfq/quote_rules.dart, seller_rfq_detail_screen.dart, seller_rfq_inbox_screen.dart, widgets/quote_copy.dart, quote_counter_sheet.dart, quote_decline_sheet.dart, and seller app English localizations.

All data is illustrative. Scenarios are alternatives, not a single inevitable sequence. Buyer offer 20 kg × ₹52 = ₹1,040; seller counter 20 kg × ₹54 = ₹1,080; listed ₹56/kg; MOQ 10 kg. Comparisons round to 7% and 4% below listed price. Linked order 1050 is fictional. Modal arrangements, helper copy, badges, toast presentation and navigation affordances are visual proposals. Status colors and generated icon variants should be standardized with actual design tokens during implementation.
