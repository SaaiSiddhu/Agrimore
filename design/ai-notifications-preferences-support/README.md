## Design basis

Informed by seller AI integration/chat, notification inbox/preferences, Settings, Help, support contact and profile screens in `apps/seller/lib/screens`, English localization strings, and `packages/agrimore_core/lib/constants/app_constants.dart`. Layouts, isolated detail screens, error specimens and document-navigation grouping are proposals, not a claim of exact shipped behavior. Platform-specific sheet styling and navigation should be finalized during Flutter implementation.

AI activation is website-only in the mobile app source. Chat content is an illustrative response, not live business analysis. API credentials are masked and fictional. Notification preferences affect push alerts while inbox entries remain. Theme supports System, Light and Dark. FAQ answers shown use project copy. Support contacts match project constants; no contact was initiated. Policy summary uses project copy; no full legal policy text was invented or verified. Account actions illustrate sign-out, not account deletion. All store, version and notification examples are illustrative. No app settings were changed.
