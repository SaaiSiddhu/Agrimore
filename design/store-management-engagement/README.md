# Foundation 22 — Store management and engagement

Nine separate Storybook-style raster concept boards for AgriMore Seller, targeting native Flutter iOS and Android. Each board contains mobile screens and supporting component states, with light and dark examples. Generated sequentially with built-in image generation; icons are raster Lucide-style illustrations, not React components or implemented Flutter widgets.

## Content and implementation notes

Fictional store Kaveri Fresh and illustrative customer, review, follower and pricing data. Visual arrangements are design proposals informed by existing seller screens, not screenshots of shipped behavior. No store settings were changed and no posts or replies were published.

Pause options are 1, 3, 7 days or until resumed; scheduled closures still apply. Keep at least one weekly day open. Holidays use Indian time and a maximum of 30 dates. Business details retain optional GSTIN. Delivery fees support flat fees or minimum-order tiers including a ₹0 starting tier. Storefront highlights allow up to three. Review replies are public and editable for 24 hours after first posting. Posts support text, an optional photo and an optional product tag.

Source areas: `apps/seller/lib/screens/account`, `profile`, `storefront`, `reviews`, and `posts`. Phone shells are illustrative; final Flutter implementation needs platform-appropriate navigation, safe areas, responsive type and actual Lucide-compatible icon assets.
